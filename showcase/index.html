Three falsifiable outcomes

The uncomfortable results are the point.

01 / Localization trap

0.922

Center prior wins

A fixed blob beats every evaluated attribution method on pointing game. It exposes dataset composition bias; localization is not causal faithfulness.

02 / Sanity failure

0.481

IG stays too similar

Integrated Gradients retains substantial similarity after head randomization. The preregistered expectation was qualitative: low is healthy.

03 / Negative result

≤0.012

The shortcut did not land

The frozen-backbone, head-only models did not learn the intended patch shortcut. That is reported plainly—not reframed as general robustness.
